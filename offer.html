Who it's for

Everyone! Whether you are brand new to group riding, coming back after a break, or training for something big, there is a session for you. You will be encouraged, never rushed.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be fit or fast?

No. Sessions are for riders of any level, and the focus is on skills and confidence rather than speed.

What if I have never ridden in a group?

Perfect, that is exactly what the skills sessions are for. We start with the basics and build up together.

How do I book?

Send Karima a message on Instagram (@karima25) and she will let you know what is coming up.

How much does it cost?

Message for details. Charity sessions such as Women Riding for Maggie's ask for a suggested £10 donation, 100% to the charity.

Come ride with us!
