About Karima

Hello, I'm Karima!

British Cycling qualified coach, London rider, and big believer that anyone can feel at home on a bike.

My story

I love riding. It has given me fitness, friends and a lot of fun, and I want to share that with others.

As a British Cycling qualified coach, I help riders build the skills that make group riding feel safe and enjoyable. Whether you are new, returning, or aiming at a first race, I will meet you where you are.

My sessions are about learning, progression and camaraderie, and about everyone leaving with a smile.

See you on the road!
How I coach

Three things I believe

Confidence first

When you trust your bike and yourself, everything else gets easier and a lot more fun.

Skills for life

Braking, cornering and riding in a group are learnable, and we practise them step by step.

Community matters

Riding is better together. Everyone is welcome, and we cheer each other on.

Giving back

Riding for Maggie's

Women-only sessions in Regent's Park, with a suggested £10 donation going 100% to Maggie's. The first session raised £160.
